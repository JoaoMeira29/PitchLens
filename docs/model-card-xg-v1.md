# Model card: PitchLens xG v1

The numbers for this model (data sizes, metrics, coefficients, calibration, penalty value) live in
[xg-v1-results.md](xg-v1-results.md), generated by `uv run pitchlens evaluate`. This card explains
the model; it does not repeat the numbers, so it cannot drift from them.

## What it predicts

The probability that a shot becomes a goal (expected goals, xG), for non-penalty shots in match
play. Penalties get a single separate value. Penalty shootout kicks are not match shots and are
never scored. Own goals are not shots.

## Data

- StatsBomb open data: Premier League 2015/16 (training), FIFA World Cup 2022 and UEFA Euro 2024
  (test). See [coverage.md](coverage.md) and [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md).
- One row per shot from the staged `shot_features` table (`pitchlens ingest`), validated by
  pandera contracts (`pitchlens validate`).

## Features

| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `distance` | metres from the shot to the centre of the goal |
| `angle` | radians the goal mouth subtends at the shot (ADR 0003) |
| `body_part_group` | foot (reference), head, other |
| `play_type` | open play (reference), set piece (from corner, free kick or throw-in), counter, direct free kick |
| `first_time` | shot taken with the first touch |

All locations are in PitchLens internal coordinates (ADR 0001); locations just past the goal line
are clamped (ADR 0002).

## Method

- Logistic regression (scikit-learn, L2 regularisation, C = 1.0).
- Split by competition, so every match is entirely in one set and no information from test matches
  reaches training. The test is deliberately hard: a league season from 2015/16 against
  international tournaments from 2022 and 2024.
- Penalty xG: the conversion rate of training-set match penalties, with its sample size reported.

## Evaluation

On the test set only: log loss and Brier score for PitchLens xG v1, StatsBomb's own xG, and a naive
baseline that gives every shot the training-set goal rate; a calibration table and plot (predicted
xG against observed goal rate by bin). The plot is written to `data/models/xg_v1_calibration.png`
and is not committed: published charts need the StatsBomb logo.

## Known blind spots

- **No defenders or goalkeeper.** Shot freeze frames are not used yet, so a shot with the keeper
  out of position looks the same as one into a crowded box. StatsBomb's model uses more
  information than ours.
- **No build-up.** Assist type (through ball, cut-back, cross) and pre-shot movement are not
  features.
- **Competition and era shift.** Trained on one league season, tested on tournaments years later.
- **Small samples at the top.** Few shots have high xG, so calibration above roughly 0.3 rests on
  small bins; read those rows with their shot counts.
- **Scaled geometry.** StatsBomb units are scaled to metres, so distances and angles are
  approximate near the goal (ADR 0001, ADR 0003).
- **Open data is a sample** chosen by StatsBomb, not all of football.
- **Penalty value** is one number from a small sample of penalties.

## Intended use

Explaining chance quality on PitchLens match pages and in write-ups, always next to the method. Not
for betting, scouting decisions or any commercial use (the StatsBomb user agreement forbids
commercial use).
